# 🛒 Shopping Mart Revenue Analysis

A data analysis project focused on understanding **shopping mart revenue and customer purchasing behavior**.

The project analyzes factors such as **products viewed, products purchased, returned products, and customer activity** to identify patterns affecting overall revenue.

### 🔍 Key Analysis

* Product-wise revenue
* Products viewed vs. purchased
* Customer purchase behavior
* Returned products
* Revenue trends and patterns

### 🛠️ Tools Used

**Python · Pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Seaborn**

### 🎯 Objective

To analyze customer and product data and identify the factors contributing to **shopping mart revenue**.
